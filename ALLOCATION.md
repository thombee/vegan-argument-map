# Root objection allocation

Each category is owned by one writer. Root ids are fixed here so writers can link across categories (`/a/<id>/`) before the target exists. Writers may add roots to **their own** category if a common objection is missing; never add a root that belongs in another category.

## moral-status: Moral status, minds and suffering
- `animals-not-conscious`: Animals aren't conscious / don't suffer like we do
- `fish-dont-feel-pain`: Fish don't feel pain
- `insects-and-bivalves`: What about insects, oysters and mussels?
- `humans-are-superior`: Humans are more intelligent, so we matter more
- `no-moral-agency`: Animals have no duties, so they have no rights
- `plants-feel-pain`: Plants feel pain too
- `where-do-you-draw-the-line`: Where do you draw the line?
- `species-loyalty`: It's natural to prioritise your own species
- `death-without-suffering`: Killing is fine if it's painless

## nature: Nature, history and necessity
- `its-natural`: It's natural; humans have always eaten meat
- `lions-eat-meat`: Lions eat meat
- `food-chain`: We're at the top of the food chain
- `canine-teeth`: We have canine teeth / evolved to eat meat
- `meat-made-us-smart`: Meat made our brains big
- `desert-island`: What if you were stranded on a desert island?
- `indigenous-peoples`: What about Inuit and other Indigenous peoples?
- `crop-deaths`: Crops kill animals too
- `wouldnt-exist-otherwise`: Farm animals wouldn't exist without us
- `overpopulation`: Animals would overrun the world if we stopped eating them
- `animals-eat-each-other`: Animals kill each other in the wild, so why can't we?

## welfare-products: Welfare, farms and specific products
- `humane-meat`: I only buy humane / free-range / local
- `small-farms`: My local farmer treats his animals well
- `backyard-eggs`: Eggs from my backyard hens hurt no one
- `dairy-doesnt-kill`: Dairy doesn't kill the cow
- `honey`: What's wrong with honey?
- `wool`: Sheep need shearing
- `leather-byproduct`: Leather is just a by-product
- `hunting`: Hunting is more ethical than farming
- `fishing-sustainable`: Sustainable fishing is fine
- `pets-and-cat-food`: Your cat eats meat / vegans own pets
- `animal-testing-medicine`: Your medicines were tested on animals

## health: Health and nutrition
- `protein`: Where do you get your protein?
- `b12-unnatural`: If you need B12 supplements, it isn't natural
- `nutrient-deficiencies`: Vegans are deficient in iron, calcium, omega-3, iodine…
- `soy`: Soy is bad for you / soy feminises men
- `children-pregnancy`: Vegan diets are dangerous for children and pregnancy
- `athletes-strength`: You can't build muscle or perform as an athlete
- `carnivore-diet`: The carnivore diet fixed my health
- `tried-and-got-sick`: I tried vegan and felt terrible
- `processed-vegan-food`: Vegan food is ultra-processed junk
- `meat-is-healthy`: Meat is healthy and nutrient-dense
- `blood-type-genetics`: My genetics / blood type need meat

## environment: Environment and land
- `grass-fed-regenerative`: Grass-fed / regenerative beef is good for the planet
- `non-arable-land`: Cattle graze land that can't grow crops
- `soy-deforestation`: Soy destroys the rainforest
- `avocados-almonds`: Avocados and almonds are worse for the environment
- `biogenic-methane`: Cow methane is part of a natural cycle
- `blame-fossil-fuels`: Fossil fuels are the real problem, not food
- `food-miles`: Imported vegan food has huge food miles
- `monocrops-palm-oil`: Vegan diets rely on monocrops and palm oil
- `manure-fertiliser`: We need animal manure to grow crops

- `preachy-vegans`: Vegans are preachy and annoying
- `too-expensive`: Vegan food is too expensive

- `religion-dominion`: God gave us dominion over animals
- `one-person-no-difference`: One person can't make a difference

- `what-if-everyone`: What if everyone went vegan?
