## The objection at full strength

I took B12, watched my iron, ate plenty of beans and tofu, saw a dietitian, and I was still exhausted and unwell. I only felt better when I added animal foods. What do you say to that?

## Response

We would say you should be believed, and that your experience needs a proper medical answer rather than a slogan. Some people have medical conditions that affect how they absorb or tolerate foods, and the supplement or dose chosen may not suit them. Only a clinician and blood tests can sort that out. The [Dietitians Australia guide](https://member.dietitiansaustralia.org.au/Common/Uploaded%20files/DAA/Resource_Library/2020/VF_A_Guide_to_Vegan_Eating.pdf) advises checking with a dietitian or doctor about supplements, and a [2026 review](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2026.1898533) stresses timely diagnosis and treatment of B12 deficiency, which can present in many different ways.

We cannot diagnose anyone online, and we will not tell you that your health should be sacrificed. If after proper investigation you find you do better with some animal foods, that is a medical judgement about you. It does not change the case for the animals.

## In short

- Doing everything right and still feeling unwell is possible and deserves proper investigation.
- A clinician can look for absorption problems, wrong doses, or other causes.
- Individual medical needs do not change the moral argument.
