## The objection at full strength

My family comes from a long line of meat eaters, possibly herders or hunters. Surely my genes are adapted to it.

## Response

This is answered in the nature section: see [It's natural; humans have always eaten meat](/a/its-natural/) and [What about Inuit and other Indigenous peoples?](/a/indigenous-peoples/). The short version is that ancestry is a weak guide to what any one person needs today, and a doctor or dietitian can test what you actually need.

## In short

- Ancestry arguments are answered in the nature section.
- Your own blood tests tell you more than your family tree.
