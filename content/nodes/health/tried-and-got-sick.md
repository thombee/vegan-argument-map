## The objection at full strength

Many people have tried a vegan diet and felt tired, foggy, hungry, bloated, cold or low. Some went to the doctor and found low iron or B12. Some got better only when they added meat back. They are often told they "did it wrong", which sounds like blaming the person. You may be one of them, and what you felt was real.

## Response

You are not imagining it, and you are not a failure. Feeling unwell on a vegan diet is common enough to be worth explaining, and the explanations are usually fixable rather than a verdict on you.

A few of the usual suspects. B12 deficiency can show up in the blood, the nerves and the skin, and a [2026 review](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2026.1898533) says it is largely preventable with timely diagnosis and treatment, which in practice means supplementing and testing. A [systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clnu.2020.11.035) found vegan diets tend to be low in B12, vitamin D, iodine, zinc, calcium and selenium. Too little food overall is another: the [Australian Dietary Guidelines](https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/australian-dietary-guidelines-2013.pdf) say strict vegetarian and vegan diets meet requirements as long as energy needs are met and enough variety is eaten. And among vegetarians and vegans in the [NutriNet-Santé cohort](https://doi.org/10.1093/jn/nxaa196), those who had been on the diet a shorter time, or started younger, got more of their energy from ultra-processed foods, which the authors say can reduce the healthfulness of a vegetarian diet.

Before you decide it can never work, it is worth seeing a doctor for blood tests and, ideally, a dietitian who knows plant-based eating. If you try again, supplement B12 from day one. If you cannot or would rather not, that is your decision; this site is about the animals, and a diet that harms you is not one we would ask you to keep.

## In short

- Feeling unwell on a vegan diet is common and often traceable to B12, iron, iodine or energy gaps.
- A doctor's blood tests and a dietitian can usually find the cause.
- If you try again, supplement B12 from day one.
