## The objection at full strength

Whatever the health case, soy is grown in places where forests are cleared, so eating soy is not harmless.

## Response

That is an environmental objection rather than a health one, and it is answered in full at [Soy destroys the rainforest](/a/soy-deforestation/). For health purposes, soy is one useful food among many; it is not required for a healthy vegan diet.

## In short

- This is an environmental objection, answered at /a/soy-deforestation/.
- Soy is not required for a healthy vegan diet.
