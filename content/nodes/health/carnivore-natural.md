## The objection at full strength

Our ancestors ate meat, we have canine teeth, and animal fat and protein built our brains. Of course a meat-based diet is going to work.

## Response

Those claims are answered at [It's natural; humans have always eaten meat](/a/its-natural/), [We have canine teeth](/a/canine-teeth/) and [Meat made our brains big](/a/meat-made-us-smart/). In short, what our ancestors ate does not tell us what is best for health or ethics today, and the evidence on health is covered on this page.

## In short

- Evolution arguments are answered in the nature section.
- 'Natural' does not settle what is healthy or right.
