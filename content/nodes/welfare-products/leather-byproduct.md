## The objection at full strength

Cattle are not raised for their skin. They are raised for meat or milk, and the hide would be thrown away if nobody used it. Wearing leather wastes less than binning it, and replacing it with plastic uses oil. If I have already accepted that the animal is dead, making use of the hide is the more respectful and less wasteful choice.

## Response

This is one of the stronger objections, and the leather industry's own trade press supports its main premise. A leather trade publication, citing the Leather and Hide Council of America, reports that in April 2020 a [steer hide was worth close to 1% of the animal's total value](https://leatherbiz.com/Features/156311), and that hides' share of the value of *by-products* had fallen to about 15 to 20%, from an average of about 58% between 1980 and 2011. The publication argues on that basis that hides are a by-product. A fair reading is that, in today's market, leather is not the main reason cattle are slaughtered.

That does not remove the objection to wearing it, for three reasons.

**It is still income.** A by-product with a price is a part of what the animal is worth to the farm. The same source shows the hide's share of by-product value has been much higher in the past, and prices move. How much is a matter of degree, but it is not nothing.

**It is the same question as meat.** If the case against killing animals for meat and milk is sound, then the demand for the hide is a small add-on to a practice that should end. Using the skin does not make the killing better.

**Alternatives exist.** Where an animal-free material does the job, there is no need to ask whether an animal's skin is "wasted".

The fairest summary is that leather is a lesser issue than meat, dairy or eggs. If you already own leather, wearing it until it wears out does no further harm. What the vegan position asks is that you do not buy new leather.

## In short

- Hides are a small share of a cow's value, and leather is not the main reason cattle are killed.
- It is still income for the industry, and part of the same practice.
- Leather you already own does no new harm; the ask is not to buy more.
