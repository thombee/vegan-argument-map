## The objection at full strength

Our ancestors hunted for hundreds of thousands of years. It is part of who we are, and hunters keep an old and honest relationship with the food chain.

## Response

This is the appeal to nature, and it has its own page: [It's natural; humans have always eaten meat](/a/its-natural/). A practice being old does not make it right, and few of us would accept the same argument for other old practices.

## In short

- Age and naturalness do not decide what is right.
- See [It's natural; humans have always eaten meat](/a/its-natural/).
