## The objection at full strength

Rather than going vegan I will eat meat a couple of times a week, and only the best quality. That gets most of the benefit without the all-or-nothing demand.

## Response

Eating fewer animal products does spare animals, and that counts. The case for and against stopping at "less" is its own root objection: [I'm cutting down; isn't that enough?](/a/reducing-is-enough/)

## In short

- Less is better than more.
- Whether it is enough is answered at [I'm cutting down; isn't that enough?](/a/reducing-is-enough/).
