## The objection at full strength

Many crops, almonds among them, rely on commercial pollination. If vegans eat those foods they are using bees as much as honey eaters are. Singling out honey is arbitrary.

## Response

Veganism is defined as avoiding exploitation and cruelty as far as is practicable and possible, not as perfect purity. That is a general point, covered at [Vegans still harm animals (phones, cars, crops)](/a/vegans-are-hypocrites/). The almond question specifically sits with [Avocados and almonds are worse for the environment](/a/avocados-almonds/).

The distinction that matters here is that honey is a product taken deliberately from bees, while avoiding crops entirely would not be possible. Where there is a straightforward substitute, as with honey, the standard is to use it.

## In short

- Perfect purity is not the standard; avoiding what we reasonably can is.
