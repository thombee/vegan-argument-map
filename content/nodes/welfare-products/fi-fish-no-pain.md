## The objection at full strength

Fish have simple brains and no neocortex. Whatever a fish does when hooked is a reflex, not suffering, so the welfare argument does not apply.

## Response

This has its own root page: [Fish don't feel pain](/a/fish-dont-feel-pain/). In brief, a review by Sneddon finds that [bony fish have nociceptors similar to those in mammals, show pain-related changes in behaviour and physiology that painkillers reduce, and show higher brain activity when painfully stimulated](https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.088823). The debate is about how to interpret that, not about whether there is any evidence.

## In short

- There is evidence from physiology and behaviour that fish respond to pain.
- See [Fish don't feel pain](/a/fish-dont-feel-pain/).
