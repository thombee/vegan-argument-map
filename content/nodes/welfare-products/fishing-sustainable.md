## The objection at full strength

I don't eat farmed meat. I eat wild-caught fish from well-managed fisheries, preferably line-caught and certified. Fish stocks are managed using science, quotas and certification, so the population is not harmed. Fish also have a good life in the open sea before they are caught. For many people fish is the healthy, ethical, sustainable choice.

## Response

Sustainability and welfare are different questions, and the objection answers only the first. "Sustainable" means the stock can replace what is taken. It says nothing about what happens to the individual animals.

Some stocks are indeed well managed, and the picture is not uniformly bad, but FAO reports that [the share of marine stocks fished within biologically sustainable levels fell to 62.3% in 2021](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-report-global-fisheries-and-aquaculture-production-reaches-a-new-record-high/en). That leaves more than a third outside those levels.

On the individual animals, the numbers are very large. One estimate puts the global catch of wild finfish at [between 1.1 and 2.2 trillion fish a year](https://doi.org/10.1017/awf.2024.7), and its authors conclude that the welfare of wild-caught fishes is generally very poor during and after capture. That figure excludes discards. Another analysis estimates that [9.1 million tonnes, about 10.8% of the global catch, is discarded each year](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-71021-x), roughly half the rate of the late 1980s, which is progress, but still waste.

Do fish feel pain? Sneddon's review concludes that [bony fish have nociceptors similar to those in mammals](https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.088823) and show pain-related changes in behaviour and physiology that painkillers reduce. The wider question has its own page: [Fish don't feel pain](/a/fish-dont-feel-pain/).

A sustainable fishery is good for the ocean. The vegan point is that it is still a harm to the fish, and the harm is avoidable.

## In short

- Sustainable means the stock survives, not that the fish are not harmed.
- Estimates suggest more than a trillion wild fish are caught a year, and the welfare of wild-caught fish is generally very poor.
- Fish have nociceptors and show pain-related behaviour; see [Fish don't feel pain](/a/fish-dont-feel-pain/).
- About a tenth of the global catch is discarded each year.
