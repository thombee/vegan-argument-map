## The objection at full strength

The farmer's animals live because he breeds them. Without his farm, they would never be born, so he is giving them a life, and that is surely a benefit to them.

## Response

This argument comes up so often that it has its own page: [Farm animals wouldn't exist without us](/a/wouldnt-exist-otherwise/). The short version is that a life you are not yet owed cannot be a favour you can bill for, particularly when the life ends early in a way that the animal would not choose.

## In short

- Creating an animal does not give the creator a right to end its life.
- See [Farm animals wouldn't exist without us](/a/wouldnt-exist-otherwise/).
