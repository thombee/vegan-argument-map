## The objection at full strength

An animal has no concept of "next year". It does not dread the abattoir the way a person would, so if the end is quick and unexpected there is no suffering and nothing lost to it.

## Response

This is a distinct root objection and has its own page: [Killing is fine if it's painless](/a/death-without-suffering/).

In brief: a person killed painlessly in their sleep, who never saw it coming, has still been wronged, because the harm is the loss of their future. The same logic applies to an animal that has a future of its own to lose.

## In short

- Not anticipating death does not make death harmless.
- The full argument is at [Killing is fine if it's painless](/a/death-without-suffering/).
