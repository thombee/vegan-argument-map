## The objection at full strength

I have leather boots and a jacket from before I thought about this. Throwing them away would be pointless, and buying plastic replacements would be worse for the planet. Should I really be made to feel guilty about them?

## Response

No. Veganism is about what you buy and use now, not about the history of your wardrobe. Wearing out the leather you own creates no new demand, and replacing it early with new products would waste resources. Second-hand leather works the same way. Most vegans take that view, though some prefer not to wear leather at all, as a matter of the message it sends.

The only point worth considering is what you replace it with. When the time comes, a non-animal option, or a second-hand item, avoids supporting new production. That is a choice for the future, not a reason for guilt about the past.

## In short

- Wearing or buying second-hand leather creates no new demand.
- Nobody needs to throw out what they own.
- When you next replace something, choose a non-animal option.
