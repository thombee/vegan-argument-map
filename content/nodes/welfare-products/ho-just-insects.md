## The objection at full strength

A bee has a brain the size of a seed. Giving moral weight to insects is absurd, and it would make every human activity impossible.

## Response

Whether insects count is its own root question: [What about insects, oysters and mussels?](/a/insects-and-bivalves/). In short, scientists disagree on how likely insect sentience is, but the New York Declaration on Animal Consciousness describes a [realistic possibility](https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/nydeclaration/declaration), and uncertainty is a reason to avoid an unnecessary harm rather than to ignore it.

## In short

- Insect sentience is uncertain, not ruled out.
- See [What about insects, oysters and mussels?](/a/insects-and-bivalves/).
