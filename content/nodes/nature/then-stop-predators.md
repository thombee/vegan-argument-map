## The objection at full strength

If animals have rights and killing is wrong, consistency seems to require stepping in when a lion kills a gazelle. Since that is absurd, the idea that animals have rights must be wrong.

## Response

This is answered at length at [Animals kill each other in the wild](/a/animals-eat-each-other/). The short version: our duty not to harm is stronger than any duty to rescue, and we owe different things for harms we cause than for harms that happen in the wild. A person can think we should not buy and eat animals without thinking we must police ecosystems we poorly understand.

## In short

- Not causing harm and preventing all harm are different duties.
