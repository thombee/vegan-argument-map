## The objection at full strength

Humans evolved in nature, so human behaviour, including farming and killing, can't be unnatural.

## Response

If everything humans do is natural, then "natural" can't separate good from bad, because it covers everything from kindness to cruelty. See [It's natural; humans have always eaten meat](/a/its-natural/), which takes up the point in detail. Naturalness can't decide a moral question.

## In short

- If all human action is natural, naturalness tells us nothing about what's right.
- See the naturalness page.
