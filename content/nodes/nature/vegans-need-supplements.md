## The objection at full strength

A diet we are really built for shouldn't need pills. The fact that vegans must supplement shows that the diet is not what nature intended.

## Response

This is a root objection in its own right and is answered in full at [If you need B12 supplements, it isn't natural](/a/b12-unnatural/).

In brief: Needing a reliable source of one nutrient does not make a diet unviable. The Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics says [appropriately planned vegan diets are nutritionally adequate](https://digitalcommons.andrews.edu/pubs/648), and planning includes fortified foods or a B12 supplement. Concede the point: B12 is non-negotiable for vegans.

## In short

- B12 is non-negotiable on a vegan diet; plan for it.
- See the B12 page for the full answer.
