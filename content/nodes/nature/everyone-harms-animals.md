## The objection at full strength

Even a careful vegan harms animals through crops, roads, buildings and products. If purity is impossible, abandoning the whole project is no worse than doing it.

## Response

This is a bridge to a larger objection, answered at [Vegans still harm animals](/a/vegans-are-hypocrites/). In short: veganism has never claimed perfection, and the standard in its definition is [as far as is possible and practicable](https://vegansociety.com/go-vegan/definition-veganism). Not being able to eliminate all harm is a reason to cut what we can.

## In short

- Perfect isn't possible; less harm still matters.
