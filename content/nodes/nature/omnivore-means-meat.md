## The objection at full strength

Fine, canines aside. Our digestion, teeth and metabolism all suit a mixed diet. That is what "omnivore" means, and an omnivore that avoids half its menu is going against its design.

## Response

Omnivore describes what we can digest, not an obligation to eat everything we can. Plenty of omnivores, including people, choose not to eat certain foods for health, religion or conscience. A mixed digestive system leaves the choice open; it does not make it for us.

There is also a gap between "can" and "ought". Humans can also eat each other, but nobody takes our ability as a reason. The deciding factor is what our choices do to others, and a pig, hen or cow feels pain and fear whether or not we are omnivores.

Finally, the claim that the omnivore design requires meat is empirical, and the dietetic evidence says otherwise: the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics holds that [appropriately planned vegan diets are nutritionally adequate](https://digitalcommons.andrews.edu/pubs/648).

## In short

- Omnivore means we can, not that we must.
- Ability to digest something says nothing about whether it is right.
