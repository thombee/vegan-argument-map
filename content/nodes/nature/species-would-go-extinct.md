## The objection at full strength

Heritage breeds are living cultural history. If the market for meat disappeared, some rare breeds might vanish with it. Veganism would then destroy diversity that farmers have spent centuries building.

## Response

The concern for rare breeds is a fair one. But there are two different things here. Species and breeds are collections; individual animals are the ones who feel pain. Keeping a breed in existence does not require that its members be killed for food. Sanctuaries, conservation programmes, seed-bank-style genetic storage and small herds kept for their own sake can maintain breeds without slaughter.

If keeping a breed alive is valuable, that is a reason to fund it directly, not to eat its members.

## In short

- Breeds are collections; the individual animals are who matter.
- Conservation doesn't require slaughter.
