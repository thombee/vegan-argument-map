## The objection at full strength

Even a few pigs or goats going feral can damage ecosystems. If animals are no longer wanted and aren't killed, some will end up loose.

## Response

That is a legitimate environmental worry about feral animals, and the answer to it is not to release them. Reduction would happen through breeding: as demand falls, fewer animals are born, and those who are already alive stay in managed care until the end of their lives. The risk the objection describes comes from abandonment, which vegans do not recommend.

## In short

- Nobody proposes abandoning animals.
- Fewer births, not release, is how numbers fall.
