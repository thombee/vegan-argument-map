## The objection at full strength

Food carries memory. For many people, particular dishes tie them to family, grief, celebration and home. Being asked to drop them can feel like being asked to drop part of yourself.

## Response

This deserves a proper answer, and it has one at [It's my culture and tradition](/a/culture-tradition/). Briefly: food traditions evolve all the time, many can be kept with plant ingredients, and the memories and the people around the table are what matter most. Tradition can explain why a practice exists but cannot by itself justify it.

## In short

- Traditions change; the table, the people and the recipes can stay.
- See the culture and tradition page for the full answer.
