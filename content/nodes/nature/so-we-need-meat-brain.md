## The objection at full strength

If our brains grew on animal foods, they may still depend on them. Choline, B12, DHA and iron are all brain nutrients richest in animal foods.

## Response

Some of these are worth planning for, and B12 in particular must come from fortified foods or a supplement. That is a real caveat and honest vegans say so. See [Vegans are deficient in iron, calcium, omega-3, iodine](/a/nutrient-deficiencies/) for detail.

What the evidence supports is that [appropriately planned vegan diets are nutritionally adequate](https://digitalcommons.andrews.edu/pubs/648), which is the position of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics. The planning is the point; an unplanned diet of any kind can leave gaps.

## In short

- B12 needs a reliable source on a vegan diet; see the nutrient page for the rest.
- A planned vegan diet is considered adequate by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics.
