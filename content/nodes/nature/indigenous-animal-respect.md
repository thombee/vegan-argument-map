## The objection at full strength

In many Indigenous traditions animals are kin, hunting is governed by obligations, and waste is taboo. That is a very different relation from the anonymity of industrial farming, and arguably closer to what vegans say they want.

## Response

There is a lot to learn here, and a vegan has no reason to be defensive. Relationships of respect, reciprocity and gratitude are far better than treating animals as units of production.

Respect for the animal, though, doesn't remove what is done to it. Vegans say that killing an animal who wants to live needs justification, and that respect is a reason to avoid doing it when we can. For communities where hunting is necessary, respect is the right way to do it. For people who can choose, it is a reason to choose otherwise.

None of this is a claim that vegans know better than people living in particular places and relationships. It is a claim about what to do where we truly have the choice.

## In short

- Respect for animals is a point of agreement, not a rebuttal.
- Respect doesn't remove the harm; it's a reason to avoid it where possible.
