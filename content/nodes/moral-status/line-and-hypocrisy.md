## The objection at full strength

You say you avoid harming sentient beings, but your life still kills insects and field animals.

## Response

That is covered in [Vegans still harm animals (phones, cars, crops)](/a/vegans-are-hypocrites/) and in [Crops kill animals too](/a/crop-deaths/). In short, the aim is to reduce harm where it is practical, not to claim perfection.

## In short

- The standard is reduction of avoidable harm, not purity.
- See the linked pages for the full answers.
