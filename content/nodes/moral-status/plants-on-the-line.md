## The objection at full strength

If you can't say where the line is, plants might be on the wrong side of it.

## Response

See [Plants feel pain too](/a/plants-feel-pain/), which covers why plants are not a plausible candidate and why the feed arithmetic holds either way.

## In short

- Plant biologists argue plants lack the structures thought necessary for consciousness.
- Eating plants directly uses fewer of them than feeding them to animals.
