## The objection at full strength

Lions don't owe their prey anything and nobody blames them, so animals' lack of moral agency shows predation is not wrong.

## Response

Lions can't be blamed, but that points the other way for us: we can. The full answer is at [Animals kill each other in the wild, so why can't we?](/a/animals-eat-each-other/), with related material at [Lions eat meat](/a/lions-eat-meat/).

## In short

- Moral agency is why we are answerable and the lion is not.
