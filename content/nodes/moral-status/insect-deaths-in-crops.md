## The objection at full strength

Even if insects matter, plant farming kills them in huge numbers, so vegans are as guilty as anyone.

## Response

This is a separate and common objection, answered in full on [Crops kill animals too](/a/crop-deaths/). The short version is that most crops are grown to feed animals, so eating animals causes more crop harvesting, not less. There is also a more general reply at [Vegans still harm animals](/a/vegans-are-hypocrites/): the aim is to cause less harm, not none.

## In short

- Most crops go to animal feed, so animal farming adds crop deaths.
- See the full answer on the crop-deaths page.
