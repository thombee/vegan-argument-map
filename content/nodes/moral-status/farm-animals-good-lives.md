## The objection at full strength

If the animal lived well and was killed well, there is nothing left to object to.

## Response

See [I only buy humane / free-range / local](/a/humane-meat/) and [My local farmer treats his animals well](/a/small-farms/). On death specifically, the first response on this page applies: even when the life is good, ending it needs a justification that a meal does not supply.

## In short

- Welfare questions are covered on the welfare pages.
- A good life does not by itself justify ending it for a replaceable benefit.
