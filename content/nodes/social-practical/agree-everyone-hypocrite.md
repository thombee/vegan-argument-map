## The objection at full strength

Everyone has a gap between what they believe and what they do: people who care about the climate fly, people who value honesty shade the truth. Nobody lives up to every value. Why should this one gap be singled out?

## Response

It shouldn't be singled out for contempt. You're right that nearly everyone has gaps, and I'm not claiming a clean record for anyone, vegan or not.

But a gap is a reason to work on it, not a licence. The argument "everyone does it" works for a trivial lapse, and weakens as the stakes rise. Here, the thing on the other side of the gap is the treatment of animals, and the action repeats every day. That also means this is a gap where small steps have an effect every day you take them.

"Hypocrisy" is also harsher than the situation needs. It is akrasia, a normal human condition, and the fix is the same as for any other: pick the most tractable piece and start there. See [Vegans are hypocrites too](/a/vegans-are-hypocrites/) for the mirror-image objection.

## In short

- Everyone has gaps, which is a reason to work on yours, not to ignore it.
- Stakes matter: this one repeats daily and falls on animals.
- Start with the most tractable piece.
