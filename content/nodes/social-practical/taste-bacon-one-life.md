## The objection at full strength

We all die. I'd rather spend my time enjoying things than doing penance. Eating well is one of the few reliable pleasures, and I won't apologise for it.

## Response

Nobody is asking for penance. A vegan life can be full of good food.

The phrase is worth turning around. "Life is short" is also true of the pig, whose life ends earlier because of the meal. If a short life makes pleasure precious, it makes each life precious.

The objection isn't wrong that enjoying life matters. It just doesn't show that this specific pleasure is one you need in order to enjoy it. You can have a good life, eat wonderful food and still decide that some pleasures cost too much.

## In short

- No one is asking for penance.
- Life is short for the animal too.
- A good life doesn't depend on this one pleasure.
