## The objection at full strength

At least I don't make up excuses or claim the animals don't matter. People who rationalise are worse than I am. Being honest about it is the ethical part.

## Response

Honest is better than rationalising, and it is rarer than it should be. Self-deception is one of the main ways the gap stays open, and you have already avoided that.

But honesty about a wrong is a reason to start fixing it, and it does little for the animals while the fixing is postponed. Think of someone who admits a debt: the admission is the right start, and it doesn't pay the debt. The honest position is also the one best placed to move, because you are not defending anything. The first steps in [the response above](/a/agree-but-still-eat-meat/) are written for exactly that.

## In short

- Honesty beats rationalising, and you have that.
- It is the start of the change, not the change itself.
- Pick one meal and begin.
