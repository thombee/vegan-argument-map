## The objection at full strength

Honestly, the way vegans talk to me is why I've never looked into it. If this were really a sound position, you'd think its supporters would be nicer about it. The behaviour puts me off the whole thing.

## Response

That reaction is understandable, and the people responsible for it owe you better. Nobody should have to suffer a lecture to get information.

But consider what is happening here. You are judging an idea by the worst people who hold it. We would not do that with medicine, environmentalism or any other subject. Rude advocates exist in every movement, including ones whose claims turn out to be correct. If you only ever met the loudest ones, you have not yet met the argument.

You can look at it on your own terms: no one watching, no one expecting a reply. Read the case for and against, see which premises you accept, and decide where you land. That puts you in charge of the process, which is probably what the lecturing took away from you.

## In short

- Disliking a person isn't a reason to ignore an argument.
- Judge the idea by its best case, not the loudest advocate.
- You can look into it privately, at your own pace.
