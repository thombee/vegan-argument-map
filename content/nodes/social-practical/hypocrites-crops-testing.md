## The objection at full strength

Combine harvesters kill field animals, and every vaccine and drug you take was tested on animals. Those aren't hypotheticals. If you accept those, you've already accepted harm to animals in exchange for convenience.

## Response

These are serious points, and each has a page of its own where the evidence is gone through: [crops kill animals too](/a/crop-deaths/) and [your medicines were tested on animals](/a/animal-testing-medicine/).

For this page the main point is structural. If crops and medicine do cause some harm, the choice is not between zero harm and some harm; it is between more harm and less. A diet built on plants uses far less cropland than one that also grows feed for livestock, as [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/land-use-diets) notes: around 80% of agricultural land goes to meat and dairy production. And medicine is a case where alternatives are limited and where most vegans reasonably take what is needed while supporting alternatives to animal testing. Food is the opposite: a place where a substitute exists.

Taking some unavoidable harm doesn't license adding avoidable harm on top.

## In short

- Each issue has its own page with the evidence.
- The choice is more harm versus less harm.
- Medicine has few alternatives; food has many.
